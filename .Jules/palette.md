## 2025-05-14 - IconButton Tooltips and Image Semantic Labels
**Learning:** In Flutter, icon-only buttons (`IconButton`) and network images (`Image.network`) often lack basic accessibility properties like `tooltip` and `semanticLabel`. These are crucial for screen readers and provide helpful context for users.
**Action:** Always check for missing `tooltip` on `IconButton` and `semanticLabel` on `Image` widgets during UX reviews.
