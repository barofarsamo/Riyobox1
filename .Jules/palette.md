## 2025-05-15 - [Accessibility gaps in interactive and visual components]
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (IconButton) and informative images (Image.network) in this application frequently lack tooltips and semantic labels, making them inaccessible to screen reader users and providing less feedback for hover interactions.
**Action:** Always check for missing tooltips on new and existing IconButtons, and ensure Image widgets have descriptive semanticLabels when they provide informational value.
